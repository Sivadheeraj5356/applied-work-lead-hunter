# Social Intent Scoring Rubric

Rubric for grading public and social intent signals:

| Intent Score | Grade | Criteria | Target Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High (Score 80-100)** | Tier 1 | Direct post by C-suite or Tech Lead asking for compute, datasets, or annotation partners. | Immediate lead enrichment and same-day draft generation. |
| **Medium (Score 50-79)** | Tier 2 | Formal job opening for infrastructure / data partnership roles, or funding PR mentioning compute scaling. | Enrich account and identify hiring manager / department VP. |
| **Inferred (Score 30-49)** | Tier 3 | Established domain player with rich proprietary operational/workflow records, but no active public solicitation. | Evaluate proprietary data licensing fit; route to Data Brokering lane. |
| **Low / Noise (< 30)** | Drop | General consumer AI discussions, opinion posts, or unverified secondary sources. | Suppress / Do not process. |
